# 04_make-tangible — build the thing people react to

One job: turn the direction into an artefact. Resolved enough to show how the experience works —
not pixel-perfect, not build-ready.

## Process
1. **Scope before making.** Decide: the key journey (the single path that proves the direction,
   not the full product) · the moments that carry the bet · the states that matter (empty, partial,
   error, edge) · what is deliberately a grey box.
2. **Choose the form** by what makes the *bet* legible — prototype, screens, workflow, storyboard —
   not by what's most familiar.
3. **Resolve the bet-carrying moments properly.** Everything else can stay loose.
4. **Name the three frames** that carry the argument on their own.

## Interrogate
- If a leader sees only three frames, which three, and do they carry the argument?
- What is least resolved — and is that because it's unimportant or because it's hard?
- Where does this rely on someone doing something they have no reason to do?
- What does this look like on day 1 with no data, and day 200 with messy real data?

## Blind spots to call out
- Happy path only — real company structures are messy
- Fidelity substituting for resolution: a beautiful screen that never says how the system knows something
- Building backwards from what's easy to prototype rather than what proves the direction

## Outputs
- Prototype / designs (linked)
- `artefact-notes.md` → `output/` — what it shows, what it deliberately doesn't, the three frames

## Human check
Hand it to someone outside the pair with no narration. They describe the bet back to you, or it
isn't resolved.
