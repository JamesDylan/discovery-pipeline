# 08_vision-horizon — lift the solution into a 12-month arc

One job: convert a solved problem into a defensible point of view about where this part of the
product is going. **This is the deliverable the project is named after.**

## Why this stage exists
The Accelerator brief produces a decision-ready direction for the next 1–3 months. That is not a
12-month vision. A well-executed three-day sprint on one problem does not change what the team
believes about whether the product has a direction. This stage closes that gap.

## Process
1. **Find the bet underneath the solution.** The direction rests on a belief about how the world
   works. Name it. *"Company structure should be inferred from behaviour, not declared by an admin."*
   That belief, not the feature, is the vision seed.
2. **Extend it 12 months.** If the belief is true and acted on consistently for a year, what becomes
   possible that isn't today? Force three horizons — **Now (0–3mo)** the Accelerator output ·
   **Next (3–9mo)** what that makes possible · **Later (9–12mo+)** what only exists once Next does.
   Each must *depend* on the previous. Independent horizons are a roadmap, not a vision.
3. **Name capabilities, not features.** What can the product *do* in 12 months that it can't today?
   Capability language survives re-prioritisation; feature language doesn't.
4. **One sentence.** Falsifiable, repeatable by a senior practitioner without a deck.
5. **Locate it in the company's AI narrative**, as recorded in the positioning section of
   `product-context.md`. If AI is incidental, say so plainly — a forced AI framing is worse than an
   honest absence. If AI is the mechanism, say specifically how.
6. **Name what you're betting against.** A vision that excludes nothing means nothing.

## Blind spots to call out
- **Roadmap in a trenchcoat** — three horizons that are just three release trains
- **Aspiration with no mechanism** — "seamless, intelligent company management", no account of how
- **Vision scoped to the sprint** — the 12-month claim is the prototype plus polish
- **Unowned** — no named person whose sphere of influence this sits in

## Outputs
- `vision-horizon.md` → `output/` — the bet · Now/Next/Later with stated dependency · capabilities
  gained · what we're betting against · open questions that would change the view · **owner**

## Human check
Say the one sentence to someone who works on the product but not on this run. Ask them to disagree with it.
If they can't find anything to disagree with, it isn't a position yet.
