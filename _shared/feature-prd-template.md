# Feature PRD template

The shape `feature-prd/02_spec` writes to. Written for an agentic delivery process: every section
is something a builder acts on.

> **STATUS: STARTER.** A generic shape. Replace it with your organisation's format, and match it to
> what `delivery-target.md` needs.

## Sections

1. **Title block** — feature name, release, owners, status, date, upstream reference.
2. **Feature** — one sentence: who can do what, that they cannot do today.
3. **Binding decisions** — references to upstream sections. Never copied text.
4. **Users and jobs**
5. **Behaviour** — main path, then every state with its outcome.
6. **Acceptance criteria** — numbered. Given, when, then. One behaviour each.
7. **Non-goals**
8. **Dependencies** — systems, teams, data.
9. **Open questions** — each with an owner and a date.

## Length

Aim for 1–2 pages. If it grows past that, it is two features.
